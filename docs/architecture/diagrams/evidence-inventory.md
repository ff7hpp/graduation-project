# Diagram evidence inventory

Complete this inventory before creating a non-trivial diagram.

| Element or relationship | Status | Supporting file/document | Notes or unresolved question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TBD | UNKNOWN | TBD | TBD |

Allowed status values:

- **VERIFIED IMPLEMENTATION** — evidenced by current source, schema, API, or executable behavior.
- **APPROVED DESIGN** — explicitly accepted in requirements, architecture, or an ADR.
- **PROPOSED DESIGN** — a candidate design not yet approved or implemented.
- **UNKNOWN** — insufficient evidence; keep as TBD.
